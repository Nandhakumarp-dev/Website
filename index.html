About

I am an experimental fluid mechanics researcher with a focus on spray dynamics and turbulence–particle interactions. I use optical laser diagnostics, including Particle Image Velocimetry (PIV), Interferometric Laser Imaging for Droplet Sizing (ILIDS) and high-speed imaging, to study combustion and multiphase flows.

I completed my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering at IIT Madras, where I was supervised by Dr. Srikrishna Sahu, and I am based in Chennai, India.

Selected publications

  1. 2026

    Droplet clustering and collective gasification in reacting and non-reacting swirling fuel sprays

    M. Balasubramaniyan, N. Pandurangan, S. Sahu · Physics of Fluids 38(9), 093324

    Description: Examines how swirling co-flow air influences droplet clustering and evaporation rate in reacting and non-reacting sprays.

  2. 2026

    Particle clustering and its role in aluminum dust cloud explosions

    N. Pandurangan, R. Inoue, A. Ueda, S. Sahu, W. Kim · Proceedings of the Combustion Institute 42, 106646

    Description: Investigates particle clustering in aluminum dust clouds and uses Voronoi analysis to predict the probability of explosion.

  3. 2025

    Dynamics of mustard seed spluttering and popping in heated cooking oil

    K. Kumar, A. Singh, N. Pandurangan, S. Sahu · Physics of Fluids 37(3)

    Description: Examines the size of the oil droplets formed when mustard seeds are cooked and presents an analytical model for the popping mechanism.

  4. 2024

    Analysis of group evaporation characteristics of droplet clusters in an evaporating spray

    N. Pandurangan, S. Sahu · Journal of Fluid Mechanics 998, A26

    Description: Analyzes the group evaporation of droplets in an acetone spray and predicts the evaporation rate of droplet groups.

  5. 2024

    Group evaporation of small- and large-scale droplet clusters in a fuel spray-laden homogeneous and isotropic turbulent airflow

    N. Pandurangan, S. Sahu · Proceedings of the Combustion Institute 40(1–4), 105434

    Description: Examines how strongly turbulence affects the evaporation of small- and large-scale droplet clusters.

  6. 2023

    Droplet cluster evolution and collective gasification of droplet groups in a fuel spray: A comparative study under non-reacting and reacting conditions

    M. Balasubramaniyan, N. Pandurangan, S. Sahu · Proceedings of the Combustion Institute 39(2), 2653–2662

    Description: Examines the formation of droplet clusters and the group behavior of droplets in reacting and non-reacting sprays.

  7. 2022

    Spatial evolution of multi-scale droplet clusters in an evaporating spray

    N. Pandurangan, S. Sahu · Physics of Fluids 34(11)

    Description: Shows that evaporation enhances droplet clustering by examining cluster formation and its degree in an evaporating spray.
